What I Learned

Redox Signaling Molecules

Your body produces molecules called Redox Signaling Molecules. You've been making them since before you were born. Every cell in your body depends on them.

They do two essential things:

They Protect

When a cell is damaged or under threat, redox signaling molecules activate your body's natural defense systems — telling the right cells to respond, repair, or replace what's been compromised.

They Communicate

They carry messages between cells — coordinating the countless processes that keep your body functioning. Healing a cut. Fighting off an illness. Recovering from a hard workout.

When the body is young and healthy, these molecules are abundant. Communication is strong. Recovery is fast. Development unfolds the way it should.

But production naturally declines over time:

~1% per year

Starting from puberty, your body produces roughly 1% fewer redox signaling molecules each year. Slowly, steadily, the network weakens.

The cells are still there. The design is still brilliant. But the messages aren't getting through the way they used to.

It's not that the body forgot what to do. It's that the body can't hear itself as clearly anymore.
