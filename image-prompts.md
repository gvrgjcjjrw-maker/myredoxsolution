# ASEA Bridge Site — Image Generation Prompts

## Concept 1: "The Daily Assault" (Distress vs. Eustress)

### 1A — Split Person Illustration
Clean, modern medical illustration style. A person standing in the center, split down the middle. LEFT SIDE: chaotic red/orange tones — icons and visual elements representing modern stressors bombarding them: factory smoke, processed food packaging, phone/WiFi signals, cleaning chemical bottles, microplastic particles, stress symbols, poor sleep (moon with X). The stressors are overwhelming, like a storm. RIGHT SIDE: calm green/blue tones — natural stressors the body handles well: sunshine, a person jogging, fresh vegetables, clean water. This side looks balanced and manageable. Between both sides, tiny glowing messenger molecules (representing redox signaling) are shown as dots of light — abundant on the right side, sparse and fading on the left. White background. No text.

### 1B — Day in the Life Timeline
Horizontal timeline illustration showing a person's day from morning to night. Clean, minimal style with soft colors. Morning: alarm clock stress, coffee (mild). Commute: traffic exhaust, phone radiation. Work: screen time, fluorescent lights, sitting. Lunch: processed food, plastic containers. Afternoon: more screens, stress hormones. Evening: cleaning products, microplastics. Night: blue light, disrupted sleep. Below the timeline, a declining curve shows the "repair signal" getting progressively weaker throughout the day. Warm, approachable illustration style — not scary, just informative. White background.

## Concept 2: "Everything Is Downstream" (Foundation/Hierarchy)

### 2A — The Tree
Elegant illustration of a tree. The ROOT SYSTEM underground is labeled or visually represents "Redox Signaling" — glowing golden roots spreading wide and deep. The TRUNK represents cellular communication. The BRANCHES hold different health interventions as leaves/fruit: peptides, stem cells, medications, supplements, vitamins, hormones, exercise recovery. The visual point: the branches and fruit look healthy ONLY where the roots below are strong. On one side, roots are thin and weak — the branches above are withering. On the other side, roots are thick and glowing — branches are thriving. Clean botanical illustration style with a modern medical twist. White background.

### 2B — The Foundation/Building
Architectural cross-section illustration. A multi-story building. The FOUNDATION at the bottom is labeled "Redox Signaling Molecules" — shown as a strong, glowing base. Each floor above represents something people invest in for health: Floor 1: Hormones & Neurotransmitters. Floor 2: Immune Function. Floor 3: Supplements & Vitamins. Floor 4: Peptides & Stem Cells. Floor 5: Medications. The LEFT side of the building has a cracked, weak foundation — all the floors above are tilting, cracking, unstable. The RIGHT side has a strong, glowing foundation — everything above is solid and aligned. Clean architectural illustration, modern and minimal. White background.

### 2C — The Operating System
Modern tech-style illustration. A smartphone or screen showing an "operating system" interface. At the base layer: "Redox OS" glowing as the operating system. Above it, app icons representing: Peptides App, Stem Cell App, Hormone App, Supplement App, Medication App, Exercise Recovery App, Sleep App. Some apps show "error" or "not responding" indicators. A notification at the bottom reads: "System resources low — base signal declining." Clean, flat design style reminiscent of iOS/Android. The metaphor is immediately clear: apps can't run properly when the OS is failing. White background.

## Concept 3: "The Decline Curve" (Supply vs. Demand Gap)

### 3A — The Widening Gap
Clean data visualization style. Two curves on a graph — no numbers, just visual. BLUE CURVE declining from left to right: "Your body's redox signal production" — starts high at puberty, steadily declines 1% per year. RED CURVE rising from left to right: "Environmental demands on your cells" — starts moderate, rises steeply representing modern life stressors. The AREA BETWEEN THE CURVES is shaded in warning orange/red — this is "The Gap." The gap widens dramatically with age. A subtle dotted GREEN line shows what happens when you supplement: the blue curve levels off or rises slightly, narrowing the gap. Minimal, elegant, almost infographic style. White background.

## Concept 4: "The Signal Getting Through" (Before/After ASEA)

### 4A — Cellular Communication
Two side-by-side panels. LEFT panel: "Declining Signal" — cells shown as simple circles, with faint, broken lines between them representing weak communication. Some cells have question marks — they don't know what to do. Muted, grey tones. RIGHT panel: "Restored Signal" — same cells but with bright, clear lines of communication between them. Cells are actively coordinating. Warm, vibrant tones. Clean, modern scientific illustration style. Not overly technical — accessible to a general audience. White background.
