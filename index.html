The Basics

What are redox signaling molecules?

You've heard of neurotransmitters — serotonin, dopamine. You've heard of hormones. These are signaling molecules. Your body runs on them. Trillions of messages every second telling cells what to do.

Now go deeper. Below all of that is a category of signaling molecule that was only recently understood — redox signaling molecules. They are the most fundamental messengers in the human body. They tell cells when to repair, when to defend, and when to replace themselves if they're too damaged to save.

Your immune response, antioxidant activation, cellular repair — all triggered by redox signals. Without them, your body is flying blind. The machinery is all there, but it doesn't know where to go or what to do.

What Goes Wrong

The signal breaks down with age.

Your body produces these molecules on its own. Peak production is around puberty. After that, production declines about 1% per year — 10% per decade.

That means by your mid-sixties, you're making roughly half of what you need. The decline is now understood to be one of the primary drivers of aging and the conditions that come with it.

This is why you can take the best supplements, eat clean, exercise, do everything "right" — and still feel like something's off. It's not the tools that are failing. It's the signal.

Think of it this way: your body still has firefighters. But the 911 call isn't getting through.
