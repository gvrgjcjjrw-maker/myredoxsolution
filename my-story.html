A Skeptic's Story

I'd taken supplements off and on over the years, but never consistently. Then I discovered ASEA. It's the only supplement I'm committed to taking for the rest of my life.

I spent my career in corporate accounting, where I learned to question bold claims and look for evidence.

Over the years, I'd seen countless products promise extraordinary results, only to disappoint. The weight loss pill that did nothing. The hair regrowth product that promised "most" people see results. Finding one that actually lived up to the hype felt like finding a needle in a haystack.

I'd reached the point where I wouldn't even try a product unless it came with a money-back guarantee. That's not disappointment — that's a protective wall built from years of broken promises.

Then my financial advisor invited me to hear a brief presentation from a medical professional about ASEA.

What caught my attention wasn't what he said. It was what he didn't say.

There were no miracle claims. No exaggerated promises. Just a straightforward explanation of something I'd never heard before — Redox Signaling Molecules. Molecules my own body naturally produces. Molecules involved in cellular communication.

That was enough for me to give it a try. My expectations were low. I honestly thought there was a good chance this would become another bottle collecting dust in the cabinet.

But there was a problem.

As I started listening to the people around ASEA, I kept hearing the same thing: "This is the greatest health and wellness discovery of our lifetime." Dr. Gary Samuelson, the atomic medical physicist behind the technology, was saying that redox signaling is destined to fuel the greatest advances in medical science this century.

I thought that was crazy. If that were true, everyone would know about it. All the doctors would know about it. I was put off. No way. Just hype that was over the top. Ridiculous to say that.

And then as I continued to take it, I found myself saying, “Are You Kidding Me?!”

It happened again and again. When I took some as my head started to show signs of discomfort — reversed. Are You Kidding Me?! When I felt a canker sore start to make itself known — reversed. Are You Kidding Me?! When my aging dog started having energy she hadn't had in years — Are You Kidding Me?!

Now I am the crazy one. Boy, was I naïve.

Then something unexpected happened.

I didn't experience one dramatic overnight transformation. Instead, over time I began noticing things that had quietly changed.

Since my early twenties, I would occasionally experience episodes that could completely derail my day. Thankfully, they were relatively rare, but as I got older they seemed to be occurring more often. Whenever I felt one coming on, I would take medication and hope I had caught it early enough.

When I began drinking ASEA, I wasn't expecting anything to change in that area. It wasn't even on my radar.

Then, sometime later, I realized something surprising. I couldn't remember the last time I'd had one.

Days turned into weeks. Weeks turned into months. Months eventually turned into years.

I can't say with certainty why they stopped. I can only tell you what I personally experienced. It wasn't something I expected, and it certainly wasn't something I was looking for.

There were other observations as well.

  • The canker sores I'd struggled with since childhood went from being a chronic, recurring problem to becoming a rare occurrence.
  • My recovery from workouts improved. At an age when many people begin slowing down, I'm in the gym six days a week, keeping pace with people young enough to be my children.
  • My cholesterol eventually returned to a normal range.
  • I kept catching myself remembering things I would usually struggle to recall.
  • Overall, I simply felt like my body was functioning better.

Then something made an even deeper impression.

My father was approaching 90 when I introduced him to ASEA.

Like anyone with an aging parent, I wanted him to enjoy as many healthy, active years as possible. I wasn't expecting miracles. I simply thought that if this product had become an important part of my life, it was worth sharing with his.

Over the years, I watched him continue to enjoy life, stay mentally sharp, and remain engaged with the people around him.

Then one day, completely out of the blue, he looked at me and said something I'll never forget.

“I think this stuff is keeping me alive.”

Those weren't my words. They were his.

Can I prove why he felt that way? No. But hearing those words from my father left a lasting impression on me. He has since passed away, but those words have stayed with me ever since.

And then there was the dog.

Our aging German Shepherd had gradually slowed down over the years, just as you would expect. Then, after she began drinking ASEA as well, she started running, playing, and showing an energy level I hadn't seen in years.

Can I explain exactly why? No. I simply know what I observed.

And dogs don't read testimonials. They don't know what they're supposed to expect. They simply act the way they feel.

Could I prove that ASEA was responsible for every one of these observations? No. And I would never make that claim. All I can honestly do is share my personal experience after nearly a decade of consistently using the product — and encourage people to do their own research and make their own decision.

That experience changed something bigger than my health.

It changed my mission.

I began studying the science behind cellular communication, oxidative stress, and Redox Signaling Molecules. I read the research. I listened to physicians, scientists, and researchers. I wanted to understand why my experience might make biological sense.

The more I learned, the more convinced I became that this wasn't just another supplement. It represented an entirely different way of thinking about health — starting at the cellular level.

Today, I don't tell people what ASEA will do for them. I can't. Every person is different. What I can tell them is what happened to me. What I observed in my father. What I observed in our aging dog. Why the science fascinated me. And why I believe everyone deserves the opportunity to learn about a technology that almost no one has heard of.

I'm not here to convince anyone.

I'm here because if someone had never shared this information with me, I would have missed out on something that has genuinely changed my life.

If you're perfectly happy with the way your body is functioning today, perhaps this story isn't for you. But if you've ever wondered whether your body is working as efficiently as it once did, perhaps it's worth taking a closer look.

Read the science. Ask hard questions. Be skeptical. I certainly was.

Then decide for yourself.
